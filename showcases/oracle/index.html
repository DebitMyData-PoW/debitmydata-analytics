01

Every block, a proof of work

Hashrate is the computing power that secures the DebitMyData chain: how many SHA-256 hashes its miners try each second, all of them together. No miner reports it; the chain tells it. Each block's difficulty says how many hashes finding it took, on average, and the time it took how fast they were tried.

            Set again at every block

              From the last 60 blocks

              Each block's difficulty comes from the times of the 60 blocks before it, the newest weighing most (LWMA): a change in hashrate shows within the hour, and one odd block can't throw it far. Each block's weight:
